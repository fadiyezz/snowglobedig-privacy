SNOWGLOBE DIG · iOS & ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Snowglobe Dig ("the app") is a puzzle game: you tilt your phone, or hold a finger near the glass, to move the snow inside a snow globe and uncover the trinkets buried in it. It is made by Studio Ekin. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

What the app itself stores

The app saves your game progress (stars per globe and the trinkets in your collection) and your settings (language, music, sound effects and vibration). All of this is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in, and it does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. It reads the phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to know which way you tilt or shake the phone; these readings are used only inside the game, on your device, and are not stored or sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner under the snow globe and on the globe list, and occasional full-screen ads between globes. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; advertising ID on Android)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions and diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data)

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool; you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy options" in the app's Settings. On iPhone the app asks for permission before it may use your advertising identifier, and you can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
